Principal Investigator

Vagheesh M. Narasimhan

Dr. Vagheesh M. Narasimhan is an Assistant Professor of Integrative Biology and Statistics & Data Science at The University of Texas at Austin. After training in Electrical Engineering, Vagheesh did a Masters in Biostatistics under Curtis Huttenhower, then a PhD at University of Cambridge and Wellcome Trust Sanger Institute under Chris Tyler-Smith and Richard Durbin. He then did postdoctoral work at Harvard Medical School with David Reich and Nick Patterson. Research funding includes Paul Allen Family Foundation, NSF, MD Anderson Cancer Accelerator, Lyda Hill Philanthropies, Love Tito’s Foundation, and BioMarin Pharmaceuticals. In 2025, he received the UT Austin Research Excellence Award.
