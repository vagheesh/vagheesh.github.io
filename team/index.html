Principal Investigator

Vagheesh M. Narasimhan

Dr. Vagheesh M. Narasimhan is an Assistant Professor of Integrative Biology as well as Statistics & Data Science at The University of Texas at Austin. After initially training in Electrical Engineering focusing on computer vision and information theory, Vagheesh did a Masters in Biostatistics under Curtis Huttenhower, and then moved to the University of Cambridge and the Wellcome Trust Sanger Institute, to do a PhD in Mathematical Genetics and Medicine under Chris Tyler-Smith and Richard Durbin. He was then a postdoctoral fellow at the Department of Genetics, Harvard Medical School with David Reich and Nick Patterson. His research work has been funded by the Paul Allen Family Foundation, the National Science Foundation, the MD Anderson Cancer Accelerator, Lyda Hill Philanthropies, Love Tito’s Foundation, and BioMarin Pharmaceuticals. In 2025, he received the UT Austin Research Excellence Award for best research paper in a calendar year across all fields.
