Research

Multi-modal AI to combine imaging and genetics to understand:

Aging

Using advanced multi-modal AI models to map how genetic variation influences age-related changes in human organs, tissues, and systems — combining imaging, molecular, and clinical data for integrative understanding.

Development and Degeneration

Integrating longitudinal imaging, transcriptomic, and genomic data to track how biological systems form, mature, and decline — identifying shared mechanisms between development and degeneration.

Disease Prediction

Building interpretable, multi-modal predictors that combine imaging, omics, and clinical data to anticipate disease onset and progression — enabling precision preventative medicine.

Using the time dimension presented by the unique power of ancient DNA to understand the human past:

Building New Computational Methods for Ancient DNA

Developing algorithms for recovering, authenticating, and analyzing degraded DNA sequences from ancient specimens — pushing the limits of what can be learned from the genetic past.

Understanding Human History

Leveraging ancient genomic time series to reconstruct migration, admixture, and adaptation across thousands of years — revealing how human populations evolved and interacted over time.
