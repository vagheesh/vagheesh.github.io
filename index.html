about

We are an interdisciplinary group at the intersection of computer science, genetics and statistics in the Department of Integrative Biology and Statistics and Data Science at UT Austin. We often generate hypotheses and candidates using some of the largest datasets available in the biological sciences, and then validate them using molecular approaches.

The main research areas of our group are: 1) Multi-modal AI to combine imaging, genetic and health record data at the scale of millions of individuals to understand the genetic basis of human evolution, development, aging, and disease. 2) Using the unique resource presented by ancient DNA which allows us to sequence human genomes from thousands of years ago to understand the genetic origin of our species.
